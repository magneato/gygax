WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Simulated quantum-adjacent interfaces: a mock inertial heading estimate informs a waypoint, while a deterministic classical simulation illustrates QKD basis sifting. The scenario releases a fictional status event after both its story link and sifting step; this is not encryption, authentication, or a secure session. No quantum hardware, channel, bits, or key is used.

SIM-TO-REAL BOUNDARY

A simulation is not machine certification. Games, races, and policies can be developed or trained in simulation, but real-machine use needs separate authorization, hardware-in-the-loop validation, independent safety review, and controlled test conditions. This demo does not train or command real machines or provide cryptographic security.
